Privacy and policy

Chatla has no server and no database. Every message travels directly between your device and your contact's device over a peer-to-peer connection.

What's stored, and where: your identity token, name and theme live in this browser's local storage. Your contacts and text message history live in this browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Photos, videos and files are sent live, device to device, and are only ever kept in memory for the current session.

The only third party involved is a public WebRTC signaling service, used solely to help two browsers find each other and open a direct connection.
